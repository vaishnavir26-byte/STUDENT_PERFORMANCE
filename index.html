Two-Stage Hurdle Architecture

In the dataset, 38 students (9.6%) scored G3 = 0 due to examination withdrawal despite passing earlier terms. A two-stage hurdle model solves this discontinuity:

Stage 1 (Completion Classifier): Test AUC = 1.0000
Stage 2 (Completer Grade OLS): Test RMSE = 0.7747
Raw 1-Stage Linear OLS: Test RMSE = 1.9201
